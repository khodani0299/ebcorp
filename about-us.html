About Us

Meet the people and experience behind Eagles & Butterflies Corporation.

Eagles & Butterflies Corporation

Bespoke development and transaction advisory services across all sectors of the economy. We give you a lift.

Eagles & Butterflies Corporation is a transaction advisory firm providing technical consulting, government grant administration and management, capital projects appraisal, project finance and syndication, BEE strategy advisory, SME development, coaching and mentorship, and enterprise and supplier development support across all sectors of the economy.

Our Founder

Founder Mr Ambani has more than 14 years’ experience in the services offered, including 11 years at the Department of Trade and Industry after joining as a Trade and Industry Advisor in 2006. His work included project screening, appraisal, due diligence, technical and credit committee review, legal vetting, site visits, fund disbursement and project closure.

Our Founder
Ambani Ramaru | Principal Strategist

The company profile records that Mr Ambani handled 54 investment projects per year on average, with project values ranging from R1 million to R200 million.

Programme experience includes EIP (2008–2014; also listed as 2008–2013), APSS (2017), SPP (2016–2017), ISP (2013–2017), TSP (2008–2013) and SMEDP (2006–2008).
