Our Services

Bespoke advisory support for businesses, investors and public-sector programmes from early screening through to execution and closure.

What we do

Clear strategic support for capital, grants and complex transactions.

EBCorp works with organisations that need more than generic consulting. Our model brings together commercial discipline, technical insight and programme delivery to help projects become fundable, implementable and investable.

01

Government Grant administration, implementation and management

Programme setup, administration, monitoring, compliance, disbursement support and implementation management for grant-funded initiatives.

02

Transaction advisory – financial, legal, technical and B-BBEE

End-to-end transaction advisory covering legal and financial structuring, technical due diligence, and B-BBEE alignment to support business and investor confidence.

03

Capital Projects Appraisal

Assessment, feasibility review and appraisal of capital projects to support funding, investment and strategic decision-making.

Full service capability

Strategic advisory across every stage of growth.

Beyond the three headline services, EBCorp supports organisations with wider project and enterprise development expertise spanning project finance, strategic B-BBEE advisory, SME and supplier development, and implementation support across public and private sector initiatives.

  • Project finance and syndication support
  • B-BBEE strategy and SME development
  • Enterprise and supplier development planning
  • Technical consulting and programme governance
